Attention weights are a softmax over a node's incoming edges of one type: they sum to one whether or not the graph changed the prediction. The contribution measure below is how much the graph moved the logit; read attention only together with it.

## Graph contribution to each prediction

`graph logit` = logit minus the logit with the graph signal set to zero (the dense layer sees `[h ; 0]`). It splits into a **day-level** part, that day's mean over all tickers, which shifts every prediction alike (market timing, invisible to within-day AUC), and a **within-day** part (`graph_logit_within_day`), which is what can change the day's ranking. Scale references: each day's cross-sectional spread (std across tickers) of the model's logit, with and without the graph signal, as medians over days.
