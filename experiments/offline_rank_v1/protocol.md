# Offline absolute-direction ranking study

Use the existing vetted 30-security archive restricted to dates before 2026 and the two prior July–December assessment windows. These are retrospective. Evaluate unchanged 1-session and 5-session absolute positive-return labels from exact exchange-session adjusted closes. For each year retain the prior 252 fit sessions and 50 threshold sessions from the outperformance study, with its unused intervening checkpoint period and five-session gaps. No model accesses the assessment for fitting, thresholds or feature engineering.

Fit four matched arms per window/horizon (16 total), seed 42. Use the established logistic C=.25 and 90-round histogram binary classifier controls. Add XGBoost date-grouped binary-relevance rank:pairwise (90 trees, depth 2, rate .05, L2 8, all pairs via mean sampling cap 30, full rows/features, CPU 2 threads). The fourth arm adds 5- and 20-session sums of one-step stock-minus-market-beta residuals and their within-date percentile ranks. Beta is estimated over 20 sessions and lagged a session. The aggregate market return is the fixed eligible cohort's equal-weight daily log return. For each raw score select a balanced-accuracy threshold among 19 quantiles on the earlier 50 sessions, tie to median quantile. Keep direction calls separate from ranking and treat all scores as uncalibrated. Diagnose high/low trailing market volatility (fit-date median) and positive/negative trailing market trend. Report paired date-block raw-score ranking intervals. No 2026 data or live operations.
