# Cohort-relative prediction and selective confidence

## Finding

The **1-session histogram boosted binary classifier** was the strongest raw ranking candidate. Its within-day AUC was **0.5284 in 2024 and 0.5273 in 2025**, ahead of matched logistic regression by 0.0271 and 0.0250. Both descriptive paired date-block intervals excluded zero. It showed no robust usable confidence threshold. It predicted one class at the calibrated 0.5 cutoff in each period, and its accuracy tied or fell below the majority baseline. Five-session results were unstable. The graph did not improve raw ranking over Price Only LSTM in either matched window.

## Target and data

For each 1- or 5-session horizon, the stock return is adjusted close at the exact future NASDAQ session divided by its signal-session adjusted close minus one. The benchmark is the arithmetic mean of those same-horizon returns over the fixed 30-name cohort, equally weighted at signal time and including the stock. The binary label is one only when the stock return is strictly greater than the cohort mean. Arithmetic averaging represents the holding-period return of an equal-weight basket formed at signal close. The benchmark is cohort-relative and has limited representation of the wider market. Missing, nonfinite or nonpositive endpoints invalidate the entire benchmark date/horizon. All scheduled endpoints needed for these windows were available. Benchmark future returns were confined to supervised labels and evaluation. Existing features use only data through the signal close.

The cohort reuses the prior audit's 30 largest 2023 median close-times-volume securities drawn from the 2023-12-29 anchor. It uses the pinned source alone with no price splice. The dataset reader filters to dates before January 2026 and verifies all 30 names on every exchange session from January 2023 through December 2025. No 2026 labels or scores were produced. The source is a retrospective adjusted-price archive. Exact historical adjustment vintages and full-cohort corporate-action coverage remain unresolved. Selection favors large liquid names.

## Fits and validation

The protocol fixed the benchmark, confidence policy and model budget before fitting. Both periods used 252 weight-fit sessions, 25 checkpoint-validation sessions and 50 calibration sessions, separated from one another and assessment by five exchange sessions. The 2024 assessment spans July 15 through December 20 (113 dates, 3,390 rows). The 2025 assessment spans July 15 through December 19 (112 dates, 3,360 rows). All learners were evaluated on identical stock-date keys and labels within period and horizon.

- **Logistic** uses the existing stock/market lagged feature family, training-median imputation and standardization, and L2 regularization with C=0.25.
- **Boosted binary** uses the same features and directly learns outperformance. LightGBM was unavailable, so the study used the existing scikit-learn histogram family with 90 iterations, seven leaves, learning rate 0.05, minimum 150 rows per leaf, L2=8 and no early stopping.
- **Boosted relative** uses the same prior histogram settings to regress cohort excess returns. The key change from earlier regression work is the primary assessment against the matching binary outperformance target, separately from absolute direction.
- **Price Only LSTM and correlation-only GAT-LSTM** use the original price-indicator sequence and BCE on the 5-session outperformance label. Matched settings were the 20-day lookback, one LSTM layer with 64 hidden units and 0.1 dropout, and Adam at 0.001 with 0.0001 weight decay. GAT used original attention with four heads on fit-only market-residual correlation edges. Checkpoints were selected once per epoch by validation within-day AUC, with four epochs of patience and a cap of 12. No news, signed messages or centering was used.

There were 16 predictor fits plus one univariate post-fit calibrator each. All used seed 42 where applicable. There were no second-seed neural fits, so neural seed stability remains unmeasured. No hyperparameter sweep was performed. The first launch exposed an unnamed date index before any fit. The runner now normalizes the axis name and completed all planned training. CPU fit and score time across all runs was about 12 seconds. Disk remained at about 10 GiB free.

## Full-population results

The 1d boosted classifier reached AUC 0.5284 and 0.5273, versus logistic at 0.5013 and 0.5023. The paired contrasts were +0.0271 [0.0112, 0.0432] and +0.0250 [0.0046, 0.0485]. Rank IC was +0.0531 and +0.0438. Weight-fit AUC was 0.6718 and 0.6597, about 0.14 and 0.13 higher than assessment. The 2024 score calibration slope was slightly negative and flipped raw ranking. The 2025 slope was nearly flat. Its calibrated accuracy was 50.18% and 47.95%, versus majority 50.18% and 52.05%. MCC was undefined for both single-class predictions. Raw ranking and the calibrated decision rule therefore give different conclusions.

The highest 5d full accuracy was 53.45% (2025 GAT), exactly its majority baseline, with MCC 0.0355 and raw AUC 0.4411. In 2024 the same GAT had 49.23% accuracy versus 50.80% majority, MCC −0.0280 and AUC 0.5078. Matched Price Only LSTM AUC was 0.5081 and 0.4554. Graph-minus-LSTM paired differences were −0.0004 and −0.0144, both uncertain. The same target has no matched earlier absolute-objective GAT fit with otherwise identical inputs, so the experiment measures graph contribution under outperformance rather than the causal effect of changing the objective on GAT. The 5d raw rank signal did not transfer. In 2025 some negative calibration slopes reversed rankings, and those calibrated AUC values are shown separately in the tables.

## Selective prediction

The confidence margin is absolute distance from 0.5 after a standardized logistic score calibration fit on the prior 50 sessions. Regressor scores go through the same mapping. The 5%, 10%, 20%, and 50% cutoffs are calibration quantiles fixed before assessment, with ties retained. These are nominal rates. The frozen thresholds can produce very different realized coverage under score drift. The 20% level is primary and 100% includes every row. Accuracy intervals use a circular 10-session date-block bootstrap, preserving overlap dependence better than independent stock-day resampling. Small subsets still give fragile intervals and point estimates.

Two individual entries crossed 70%. Both came from 2025 1d logistic. At nominal 10%, one prediction on one date was correct, matching the 100% subset majority. At nominal 20%, six of seven predictions were correct (85.71%) across seven dates and five stocks. Actual coverage was **0.21%**, with three positive and four negative calls. Its selected-subset majority was 71.43%, and the descriptive accuracy interval was 50% to 100%. This is one additional correct call over the subset majority. The same rule in 2024 selected 782 rows (23.07%) and scored 50.64% against 53.45% majority. There is no meaningful, repeatable 70% result.

The most notable less-sparse selective result was 2025 5d GAT at nominal 5%. It reached 66.53% versus 63.56% subset majority, with 505 predictions across 109 dates and 15.03% actual coverage. It issued 69 positive and 436 negative calls. The 95% accuracy interval was 57.08% to 74.64%. The paired superiority interval also included zero (approximately −1.38 to +6.79 percentage points using the same date-block length). In 2024, its same-policy result was 55.91% on 93 all-negative calls, tied with the subset majority. The 1d boosted ranking candidate did not yield a better abstention rule. At nominal 20%, it achieved 49.24% on 792 rows versus 50.76% majority in 2024 and 46.85% on 254 rows versus 53.15% majority in 2025.

## Absolute-direction diagnostic and decision

As a separate diagnostic, saved outperformance calls were evaluated against strictly positive absolute returns from the same adjusted prices. Raw outperformance scores were ranked against that original target. There was no direction refit, recalibration or threshold selection. Best diagnostic AUC was 0.5368 from 2024 5d Price Only LSTM, with accuracy of 50.59% against 52.18% majority. Best diagnostic accuracy was 55.03% from 2025 5d logistic versus 54.46% majority, alongside 0.4492 raw within-day AUC. The 1d boosted classifier's absolute-direction AUC was 0.5226 and 0.5127, and its calls failed to beat the direction majority baseline. These mixed findings do not establish an improvement on the original task. Official GAT-LSTM direction metrics remain separate.

Retain the **1d boosted binary model as an exploratory ranking candidate**, with its fixed settings and raw scores. The matched gains against logistic occurred in both windows, but the strong fit-to-assessment gap and weak calibration argue against deployment or a confidence operating point. There is no supported 5d winner. Independent validation remains blocked by the incomplete frozen 101-security data and the 2026 scoring lock. Both periods here informed earlier development and cannot independently validate model selection.
