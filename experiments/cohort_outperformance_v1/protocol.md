# Cohort-relative prediction protocol

Use the previously defined 30 most liquid 2023 anchor securities and the pinned price archive only. Read strictly before 2026. Require complete exchange-session coverage for the cohort from January 2023 through December 2025. For horizon 1 or 5, the benchmark is the arithmetic mean of the 30 adjusted-close returns from signal close through the exact future exchange close, equally weighted at signal time and including the predicted stock. Outperformance is strict excess of the stock return over that mean. If any cohort endpoint is missing or invalid, the whole benchmark date/horizon is undefined. Do not replace securities or fabricate exits. The original strictly positive return computed from the same prices serves only as a secondary diagnostic.

Windows are July 15 through December 20, 2024 and 2025 on actual exchange sessions. Before each assessment use 252 weight-fit, 25 checkpoint validation, and 50 calibration sessions. Leave five trading sessions between successive partitions. The original archive overlap and audit qualify the prior 30-name study for exploratory development only.

Fit regularized logistic C=0.25, histogram gradient boosting classifier and the prior 90-round histogram relative-return regressor at both horizons in both periods. LightGBM is absent. Compare correlation-only original GAT against Price Only LSTM on the 5-session target in both periods with the same seed, prices, labels and partition rules. Fixed seed 42. This uses 16 predictor fits. Each fit has a separate univariate post-fit score calibration. Stop at this design. No graph tuning and no 2026 scoring.

Use raw scores for within-day AUC and mean daily Spearman rank IC. Fit a standardized logistic calibration mapping on the 50 calibration dates and classify at probability 0.5. At nominal 5, 10, 20 and 50 percent levels, freeze the calibration quantile of absolute calibrated margin from 0.5 and apply it globally to the assessment scores, including ties. Report actual rates and date-block intervals. Report all rows at 100 percent. The 20 percent rule is the primary selective operating point. Models are compared on retrospective data only. Secondary evaluation uses raw scores and frozen outperformance calls against absolute direction without recalibration or fitting to that task.
