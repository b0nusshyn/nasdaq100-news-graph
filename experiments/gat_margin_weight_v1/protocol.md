# Fit-only weighting by magnitude

Near-zero realized return signs may be noisier supervision than larger moves. This differs from pairwise ranking and from using a continuous target. Keep the original binary target and every eligible row. Within each weight-fit session, rank absolute realized adjusted forward return, average ties and assign weights 0.75 + 0.50 × percentile. Normalize by the sum of labelled batch weights. Build weights only for eligible weight-fit keys and reject unweighted target rows in the fit objective. Validation and assessment use ordinary metrics and unweighted BCE checkpoint loss. No weighting of evaluation rows, no learned weight selection. Compare 64-unit BCE control and 32-unit BCE control on identical historical rows, original graph and threshold policy. Both horizons, both periods, seeds 42 and 43. No 2026 or notebook operation.
