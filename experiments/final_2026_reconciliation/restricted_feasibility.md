# Restricted-cohort feasibility

The earlier 30-security subset selected on 2023 median dollar volume has complete bars in the pinned archive through its 2024 and 2025 development windows. That subset favors large liquid securities and cannot establish performance for the fixed 101-security cohort, historical index membership, or acquired names. A drop-in 96-name survivor cohort would additionally condition on observation and survival through November 2026. The missing histories include completed exits, so this selection is structurally related to the outcome universe.

The previous restricted study remains suitable as descriptive development evidence. Its relative-return model's 1-day AUC advantage was 0.0112 in 2024 and 0.0058 in 2025, with paired intervals crossing zero. The best full-population result was 54.46% accuracy against a 54.46% majority baseline, and the best within-day AUC was 0.5411, both in the 2025 5-day logistic cell. The 63.64% selective result was 22 all-Up predictions, 0.65% coverage, and exactly matched the subset majority. [Prior report](../post2023_validation_v1/report.md)

No additional restricted fit or assessment was run. Substituting or redefining a cohort after this reconciliation would require an explicitly separate protocol and a separate future window before making an independent claim.
